# TXU Brand System

## Primary idea

TXU is pronounced **thank you**. Its final mark uses a compact human gesture in
warm coral and dark ink: two abstract forms meet around a small white diamond,
suggesting appreciation moving from one person to another. The symbol remains
recognizable at favicon size without relying on a literal receipt icon or a
mechanical letter construction.

The warm coral represents the human act of recognition. Dark ink and warm paper
support TXU's trustworthy written-record character without making the product
look like a payment application.

original shapes, colors, and spacing rather than reconstructing the logo in
